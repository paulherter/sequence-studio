## Deviations and decisions
- The v8 -> v9 corner-radius fold runs when a `Track` is read (`TrackWire::try_from`), not in `Clip`'s own decoder (serde
  derive has no hook; a wire copy of its 40 fields is not worth it). A `Clip` decoded on its own keeps
  `animation.legacy_corner_radius`. The new effect gets `Id::NIL` (Swift draws a random id).
- `SpeedKeys` recomputes its boundary table on each query (Swift caches it behind `didSet`); the fields stay public as in the
  skeleton. Marked `shortcut:` in `speed_keys.rs`. Segments per clip are few; upgrade when a profile asks.
- `EaseShape::effective` / `linear` / `hold` are the keyframe-editing task's. `speed_keys_ease.rs` carries a private copy of
  the two rules it needs (`is_effectively_bezier`, `linear_shape`); point them at the shared ones after the merge.
- `GraphicsDocument::remove_layers` in `graphics_item_range.rs` is a private copy of Graphics' `remove` (`cropping_item_ranges`
  needs it); replace it with the Graphics one after the merge.
- `Timeline::can_retime` and `retime_group` (Timeline+Speed.swift) live in `timeline.rs` because `edit_speed_keys` needs them.
- Edits that mint ids take `&mut dyn IdSource`: `Track::overwrite/clear/insert/split` (+ `*_relinking` variants with the shared
  old-link to new-link map), `Timeline::default_tracks`, `set_master_volume_key`.
- Swift `internal` members used by other crates are `#[doc(hidden)] pub`: `Clip::extend_head/extend_tail/rescale_keys/cropped/
  relink`, `Timeline::find/linked_group/involved_tracks/following_straddlers/retime_group`, `Member`.
