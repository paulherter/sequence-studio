## Core pieces written locally (delete when the core has them)
- `timeline_captions.rs`: the Swift `Timeline` caption verbs that touch an item list (`Captions.swift:401-570`:
  replaceCaptions, addCaptionTrack, split/merge/add/delete/replace text, style/animation/position setters, karaoke).
  Only the read side and the item-list operations are in `sq-core-audio`. New tracks are built through JSON because
  `CaptionTrack::items` is private (same shortcut as `sq-core-effects/sequence_conform.rs`).
- `common.rs`: `es5_perform`, `es5_perform_timeline`, `es5_attempt`, `es5_message` repeat ES2's `perform_timeline` /
  `attempt` / `message_for`; `discard_text_clip` repeats ES1's `rewind_undo`; the lift of an empty text clip removes the
  clip from its track (Swift `TimelineEdit.lift`); `add_tone_clip` writes out `Project.addToneClip`.
  After the merge replace them with the ES1/ES2 versions.
- `session.rs` `perform_steps` (the `shortcut:` spot naming ES5): `resolve_text_tokens` runs after every edit.

## Deviations / decisions
- Needs of the Cargo.toml (core file): `sq-text`, `serde`, `serde_json` as dependencies (ES4 adds `sq-text` and
  `serde_json` too; identical lines).
- Ids for new objects come from `self.ids`; the pure builders take `&mut dyn IdSource`: `CaptionMapping::items*`,
  `CTAEndCard::layers`, `GraphicPresetEntry::layers`.
- `CaptionLanguageGuess::retry_language` takes the recognizer as a closure `detect(text) -> (code, confidence)`
  (Apple's `NLLanguageRecognizer` is the app's).
- `CaptionTranslator` (Apple Translation) is the trait `CaptionTranslateAsks` the app implements next to `SessionRoot`;
  `translate_captions` is `async fn translate_captions<R: SessionRoot + CaptionTranslateAsks>(h, target)`.
- `CaptionLookLibrary` works over the trait `CaptionLookStore` (ES4's `LookStore` implements it); the default look id
  lives in a `SettingsStore` under `sq.captionLook.default`. Not ported: the copy into the old preset store (`legacy`)
  and the follow of a default look merged into another Look (both belong to the Look store).
- `CaptionMonitorHit::edit_target` returns `CaptionEditTarget` (clip, centre, box, style) instead of Swift's
  `OverlayTarget` (ES2, `SnapGuides.swift`, not declared yet).
- `CaptionPosition::default()` is `common::default_caption_position()` (the one in `sq-text` is crate-private).
- Graphic presets are `GraphicPresetCatalog::all()` clones of a `LazyLock` list; the entry builders take an id source.
